About us

A general practice for adults and children in Lagos, built around unhurried appointments.

Our doctors see patients for everyday health concerns: check-ups, coughs and fevers, blood pressure, and advice on what to do next.

Every appointment is 30 minutes with one doctor, so there's time to talk properly. You book online and arrive at your time, instead of waiting in a queue.
